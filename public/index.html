What do mild, moderate, and strong mean?

Mild means brief, lower-intensity, or lightly referenced content.

Moderate means clearer, more explicit, recurring, or more intense content.

Strong means graphic, intense, central, or especially distressing content.

Illustrative examples: mild violence might be a brief, non-graphic scuffle; moderate violence might include repeated fights or visible injury; strong violence might include graphic injury, torture, or killing. Mild language might be occasional mild profanity; stronger levels may be repeated, severe, or pervasive.

For sexual content, substance use, and self-harm, context matters especially: a passing reference is different from recurring, explicit, or central content.

These examples are guidance, not strict definitions or universal ratings.
